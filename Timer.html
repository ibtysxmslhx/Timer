Screen Detox & Mind Relaxation Guide

Quick, scientifically proven methods to restore eye freshness and boost study focus.

20-20-20 RULE

Look Out the Window (20-20-20 Rule)

Turn your gaze away from screens. Locate an object 20 feet away (or outside the window) and focus on it for 20 seconds to relax your eye lenses.

WARM PALMING

Warm Palm Palming Technique

Rub your palms together until warm, then gently place them over your closed eyelids to stimulate refreshing blood circulation around the optics.

COGNITIVE RELIEF

4-7-8 Diaphragmatic Breathing

Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7 seconds, and exhale slowly for 8 seconds to actively reduce cognitive stress and calm your mind.

BRAIN HYDRATION

Drink Water for Brain Oxygen

Drink a glass of fresh water and stand up. Keeping your brain hydrated prevents sleepiness and fatigue during high-focus tasks.
